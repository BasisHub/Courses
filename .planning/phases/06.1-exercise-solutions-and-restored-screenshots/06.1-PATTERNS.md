## Shared Patterns

- **Header and BUI redirect:** every DWC solution (excerpt above). Intro-bbj chapter 1/2 solutions omit it (client-neutral, D-10).
- **File tail:** `declare auto` lines, then `use` lines, at the bottom of DWC samples (Grid analog lines 118-134).
- **Closing:** `window!.setCallback(BBjAPI.ON_CLOSE, "onExit")` plus `onExit:` / `release`.
- **Download link and ZIP:** `pathname:///files/<book>/<zip>`; ZIPs are produced by `tools/sync-samples.py` (`--check` in verify). Existing ZIP layout: `docs/static/files/dwc/<module>.zip`, `dwc-samples.zip`; `docs/static/files/intro-bbj/*.zip`, `intro-bbj-samples.zip`.
- **Verification gates:** `bbj_check_syntax` per file, results into `tools/data/dwc-samples-syntax.md` and `tools/data/intro-bbj-syntax.md`; Vale; `npm run build`.
- **Prose:** no em dashes (checker line 371 enforces), second person, front matter `title` starts with "Exercise: ", description 1 to 160 characters.
